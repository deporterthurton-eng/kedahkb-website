Our Heritage & Capability

A Proven Global Partner in Commercial & Residential Sanitary Hardware

Fujian Kedah Kitchen & Bath Co., Ltd. (target domain: kedahkb.com) is located in the heart of China’s renowned Plumbing Industrial Area in Lucang Town, Nanan City, Fujian Province. With over 30 years of specialized manufacturing experience, Kedah has grown into a modern, integrated sanitary enterprise spanning over 50,000 square meters of specialized production facilities.

We specialize in the research, design, casting, machining, surface finishing, and assembly of premium lead-free brass faucets, intelligent touchless sensor taps, commercial flush valves, and luxury thermostatic shower systems.

As an ISO 9001:2015 certified manufacturer with North American IAPMO cUPC listing (File No. 8933) and European CE conformity, we are the trusted supply chain partner for leading plumbing brands, engineering contractors, real estate developers, and wholesale distributors across the USA, Canada, Spain, Saudi Arabia, Colombia, and Thailand.
