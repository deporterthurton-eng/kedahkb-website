Quality & Craftsmanship

Our 7-Step Precision Manufacturing & QA Process

Every Kedah faucet and valve undergoes 7 rigorous manufacturing stages to ensure zero leakage, smooth cartridge actuation, and immaculate surface aesthetics.

01

Precision Gravity Casting

High-purity solid brass gravity casting eliminates sand holes and internal porosity, creating robust heavy-duty valve bodies.

02

High-Precision CNC Machining

Automated CNC lathe machining with tolerance controlled within 0.01mm for flawless cartridge fit and water-tight threading.

03

Ultra-Fine Surface Polishing

Multi-grit robotic and manual mirror polishing creates a smooth, ripple-free surface essential for high-adhesion electroplating.

04

In-Line Visual & Dimensional QC

100% pre-plating inspection to detect micro-scratches, burrs, or dimensional discrepancies before surface treatment.

05

Clean Room Precision Assembly

Equipped with premium Sedal/Wanhai ceramic cartridges (500,000 cycle lifespan) and Neoperl aerators for smooth laminar water flow.

06

100% Air & Water Pressure Test

Dual testing with 0.8 MPa hydrostatic pressure and 0.6 MPa air pressure on every single assembled unit to guarantee 0% leakage rate.

07

24H ASS Salt Spray Testing

Automated plating lab testing with 24-hour Acetic Acid Salt Spray (Grade 10 rating) ensuring superior corrosion resistance.
